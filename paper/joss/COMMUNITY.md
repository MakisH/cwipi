# Community and Support

Welcome to the CWIPI community! Whether you are a user or a developer, here is how you can interact with us.

## Support and Assistance
If you encounter difficulties using CWIPI or have questions about its features:
* **GitHub Issues:** Please use the [Issues section](https://github.com/onera/cwipi/issues) of this repository. This is the primary channel for technical support and usage assistance.
* Before opening a new issue, please search the existing ones to see if your question has already been answered.

## Development Ecosystem
CWIPI is a research-grade coupling library developed by ONERA. While its developments aim to meet both internal requirements and the needs of the aerospace research community, we highly value community feedback:
* **Roadmap:** Core developments are defined by the ONERA development team. Major proposals from the community are discussed via GitHub Issues.
* **Internal Mirroring:** Be aware that there may be a slight delay between internal developments and their public release on GitHub.

## Code of Conduct
We are committed to providing a friendly and inclusive environment. Please be respectful in all your communications.
